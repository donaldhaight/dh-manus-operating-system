# Construction Management Founder Questions and Open-Decision Register

**Status:** Required decisions and evidence requests. A question is not a requirement until a named owner makes it a decision. “Unknown” is a valid answer at this stage.

## The five gates that must be answered first

| ID | Founder decision | Why it cannot be deferred |
|---|---|---|
| **CM-001** | Who is the first user, separately from the buyer, approver, payer, and beneficiary? | “Construction management” is not a usable persona. |
| **CM-006** | Which single internal handoff or exception will be observed? | The first proof cannot serve every restoration workflow. |
| **CM-017** | Which state, project type, delivery model, and role boundaries apply? | Authority, licensing, adjusting, and contract analysis are context-specific. |
| **CM-028** | What sources may be used, with what authorization, retention, and deletion rule? | No proof can safely begin without data-rights clarity. |
| **CM-045** | What result would cause the team to stop rather than expand? | Prevents an attractive narrative from replacing evidence. |

## Completion rule

The package can move from research to a controlled proof only when CM-001, 006, 017, 028, and 045 have decision-owner answers, and when the proposed activity does not require real data, external communication, claim action, or production integration outside approved human gates.
