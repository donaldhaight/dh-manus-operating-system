# Construction Management Stakeholder Group — Working Package

## Purpose

This package is the second stakeholder-group research program within the Human Blockchain / InsuranceRestorationMarket beachhead. It examines Construction Management as though a new venture seeks to earn sustained preference in a **narrow, lawful, and measurable coordination category**. It does not assume that Human Blockchain owns, represents, regulates, or can universally standardize the construction-management market.

The package begins from the existing beachhead rule: **Construction Management is a core operations/domain group, but it should appear publicly only through defined restoration workflow and service capabilities.** It is neither a generic directory category nor evidence of a completed stakeholder affiliation.[1]

## Status and boundary

| Item | Current position |
|---|---|
| **Research stage** | First market-research pass complete; source validation, stakeholder dossier, alternatives/SWOT, decision register, and controlled proof plan are present. |
| **Geography** | United States, subject to state/local and contract-model variation. |
| **Initial market lens** | Insurance-restoration project coordination and construction-management handoffs. |
| **Starting thesis** | A proposed evidence-to-decision layer may improve one high-friction cross-party construction/restoration handoff if it makes authority, source evidence, exception handling, and service expectations legible. |
| **Explicit non-claims** | Not a construction manager, general contractor, insurer, adjuster, public authority, legal adviser, claims handler, referral network, marketplace, certification program, or guarantee of project outcome. |
| **Decision rule** | No majority-share, market-dominance, interoperability, standard, affiliation, or revenue claim before a defined customer/problem pair, authorized proof loop, and repeat-use evidence exist. |

## Cross-group rule

Construction Management is the operational proving ground; VC/PE–VentureTech is a capital and authority lens. The construction package must not shape itself around investor narrative alone. Instead, any later capital-layer argument must be earned through construction/restoration users’ demonstrated workflow utility, disclosed governance, and repeatable evidence.
