# Construction Management Market-Leadership Research Constitution

**Reference date:** September 5, 2026
**Status:** Founder-facing research constitution. The core market definition is a proposed working boundary, not an approved commercial, legal, or product decision.
**Research objective:** Determine whether Human Blockchain / InsuranceRestorationMarket can earn defensible, repeatable decision utility in one construction-management coordination category before any broader market, affiliation, standard, or platform claim.

## 1. Decision to be informed

The decision is not whether to “enter construction management” broadly. The decision is whether a bounded Construction Management offer can solve a real, high-friction **insurance-restoration coordination problem** better than the customer’s current combination of people, documents, spreadsheets, email, phone calls, job-management software, general contractor processes, carrier/adjuster processes, and informal trust networks.

> **Working market definition:** A proposed, human-accountable evidence-to-decision coordination service for U.S. insurance-restoration construction-management workflows where multiple legal entities must resolve a defined handoff or exception using attributable source evidence, named authority, visible service expectations, and a human decision path.

### Explicit exclusions

| Exclusion | Why it is excluded at this stage |
|---|---|
| General construction-management software market | Too broad; mixes new construction, infrastructure, commercial, residential, and restoration needs with different buyers, contracts, and workflows. |
| General-contractor replacement | The program is not a builder, contractor, construction manager, or project-management provider. |
| Claims adjudication, coverage interpretation, or settlement | These can be regulated, contractual, insurer-controlled, and legally sensitive. |
| Public directory, lead auction, or provider ranking | Discovery is not proof of capability, and paid placement/ranking would corrupt early evidence. |
| Broad “industry standard” or certification | A vocabulary or metric is not a standard/certification without a real governance, rights, evidence, appeal, and operating structure. |
| Autonomous agent action on projects or external systems | Agents may assist research/drafting only within explicit authorization; consequential action remains human-gated. |

## 2. Working stakeholder and workflow vocabulary

The labels below are **research terms**, not a final Human Blockchain ontology or statement of legal authority. Primary-source research must distinguish construction-manager delivery models, general-contractor obligations, owner rights, contractor/subcontractor duties, insurer/adjuster functions, and jurisdiction-specific requirements.

| Research object | Working meaning | Research test |
|---|---|---|
| **Construction Management** | The role(s), organization(s), and processes responsible for planning, coordination, schedule, cost, quality, documentation, and delivery of project work. | Identify which functions apply in restoration and who actually holds authority. |
| **Restoration handoff** | A point at which a decision, evidence package, task, scope, schedule, change, authorization, or exception crosses organizational boundaries. | Identify the exact current and desired state. |
| **Evidence-to-Decision packet** | A bounded record connecting source evidence, actor/entity/role, proposal, service expectation, decision, exception, and learning. | Determine whether participants find it decision-useful and proportionate. |
| **Authority context** | The attributable entity, role, permission/delegation, scope, conflict, and human escalation path relevant to a proposed action. | Verify that authority is not inferred from access to data or a platform login. |
| **Proof loop** | An invitation-only, authorized/synthetic or otherwise appropriately governed test of one workflow. | Measure usefulness, evidence completeness, handoff clarity, rework, exception resolution, and participant willingness to repeat. |

## 3. Market-leadership test

“Majority share” cannot mean control of construction management, claim authority, industry governance, or broad market influence. For this research, it means a future, evidence-based possibility of **sustained preference for one narrowly defined coordination job** among a named and permissioned early cohort.

| Required question | Evidence needed before treating the answer as true |
|---|---|
| Who is the first user and accountable buyer? | Interview or authorized field evidence that distinguishes user, buyer, approver, and beneficiary. |
| What is the single costly/frustrating decision or handoff? | A current-state workflow with participants, artifacts, delays/rework/risks, and conflicting incentives. |
| What alternatives are used today? | Buyer-centered map of existing software, internal processes, consultants, association guidance, and do-nothing behavior. |
| What exactly is the first offer? | One defined output, input boundary, service level, delivery owner, pricing hypothesis, and explicit exclusions. |
| Why would a participant change behavior? | Observable time, clarity, risk, rework, evidence, or coordination advantage—rather than a generic technology claim. |
| Who may act or decide? | Entity/role/authority model, human review path, consent/data-rights limit, exception/appeal rule. |
| How will success and failure be measured? | Baseline, numerator/denominator, cohort, date range, data permissions, independent review, and stop/revision rule. |
| What could make the model unsafe or nonviable? | SWOT, contracts, insurance/claims sensitivity, privacy, procurement, licensing, labor, local-practice, cybersecurity, and economic-risk review. |

## 4. Research questions by lens

### Founder and market lens

The research will identify the smallest customer/problem pair that could support a meaningful startup wedge. It will distinguish commercial value from an attractive narrative and document the customer’s existing alternatives, purchase path, switching friction, and reasons not to adopt.

### Operator and workflow lens

The research will map where restoration construction work changes hands, who owns each decision, how scope/schedule/change/evidence exceptions are handled, which source systems are used, and where a neutral evidence packet might help rather than duplicate existing project-management tools.

### Capital and economic lens

The research will separate value recipient, economic buyer, sponsor, payer, beneficiary, and party bearing risk. It will not assume that the party benefiting from better coordination is willing or authorized to pay. No TAM/SAM/SOM, price, market-share, or return figure will be presented until the market boundary, commercial unit, and source inputs are approved and documented.

### Legal, insurance, safety, and reputation lens

The research will examine authority boundaries and decision risks without giving legal, insurance, contract, coverage, licensing, employment, procurement, or financial advice. It will identify which questions require qualified counsel, insurer/carrier, privacy, security, or contract review before a public or operational claim can be made.

### Technology and data lens

The research will evaluate where existing tools are systems of record, systems of action, or sources of evidence. It will test whether a Human Blockchain layer adds attributable coordination rather than becoming another dashboard or ungoverned agent. Any future API, MCP, integration, or automation work must remain read-only/scoped until explicit approved tool/data and human-gate criteria are met.

## 5. Evidence constitution

| Evidence status | Meaning | Publication / decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Observed** | Directly confirmed in primary legal, regulatory, standards-body, association, company, public-procurement, or authorized workflow source. | Cite the source, date, scope, and limitation. |
| **Reported** | Credible third-party or field material not directly revalidated. | Use only as a lead or clearly qualified context. |
| **Inferred / proposed** | Reasoned Human Blockchain interpretation or potential offer. | Requires field validation and a named decision owner. |
| **Founder decision required** | A choice concerning scope, commercial position, governance, risk tolerance, or investment. | Do not let research silently convert it into a decision. |
| **Counsel / qualified review required** | Material legal, insurance, privacy, security, licensing, or contractual question. | Hold any public/operational claim until appropriate review. |

The source hierarchy is: applicable law/regulator/public authority and executed/authorized project evidence; then standards bodies and professional associations; then company documentation and public filings; then peer-reviewed/credible research; finally trade media and opinion. Search results, AI outputs, marketing copy, generic directories, and social posts are discovery aids, not final authority.

## 6. Research lanes for the first market pass

| Lane | Decision it informs | Initial primary-source targets | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Delivery models and authority** | Which CM/GC/owner/contractor roles actually hold which project responsibilities? | Professional associations, standard-setting organizations, public owner guidance, statutes/regulators as applicable. | Role/authority map with scope warnings. |
| **2. Restoration workflow and handoffs** | Which cross-party handoffs or exceptions are suitable for a bounded proof? | Authoritative restoration/industry bodies, documented workflows, authorized field material. | Current-state handoff map and candidate proof loops. |
| **3. Buyer, payer, and incentives** | Who sees pain, who pays, and who can block adoption? | Public company/industry disclosures, association research, authorized interviews. | Stakeholder-incentive and value-exchange map. |
| **4. Alternatives and technology landscape** | What do users do instead, and where is a coordination layer genuinely differentiated? | Official product documentation, public customer materials, standards/association resources. | Buyer-centered alternatives map; adopt/adapt/reject matrix. |
| **5. Commercial and adoption pathway** | What first offer, channel, proof artifact, and referral/retention motion may be viable? | Public procurement/research, provider documents, authorized interviews. | Early-offer and adoption hypothesis with falsifiers. |
| **6. Risk, governance, and resilience** | Which contracts, data rights, licensing, insurance, security, and local-practice limits shape the offer? | Regulators, legal texts, safety bodies, authoritative guidance. | Risk register and human-gate requirements. |

## 7. Deliverable contract

The Construction Management package will contain a sourced market/stakeholder dossier, workflow/alternatives map, explicit SWOT, requirements and open-decision register, and market-leadership/proof plan. Every factual claim will have an inline citation or be labeled as reported/inferred/proposed. Every proposed feature or process will declare its source basis, owner, user/beneficiary, authority limit, data boundary, success measure, failure condition, and required review.

## 8. Draft launch gates

| Gate | Minimum condition | Result if not met |
|---|---|---|
| **G0 — Scope** | Founder approves the initial user/problem/geography and exclusions. | Do not calculate market size or claim a category. |
| **G1 — Authority** | Role/decision ownership, data rights, and prohibited actions are documented. | Do not ingest real project/claim data or automate external work. |
| **G2 — Utility** | A controlled artifact helps a qualified participant make a clearer or more timely decision. | Revise the job/problem or end the proof loop. |
| **G3 — Repeat use** | Independent participant reuse, correction, contribution, or follow-on request exists. | Do not introduce paid scale, a broad directory, or affiliation language. |
| **G4 — Economic integrity** | Payer/beneficiary/conflict and disclosure model are approved; counsel review occurs where needed. | Do not accept paid placement, referral compensation, or investment-adjacent monetization. |
| **G5 — Narrow leadership metric** | A repeat-use metric within a defined cohort is auditable and meaningful. | Do not claim majority share, standard setting, or broad authority. |

## 9. Open founder decisions

| ID | Decision | Default until decided |
|---|---|---|
| CM-001 | Which exact first construction-management user is in scope: restoration operations manager, project manager, owner representative, general contractor lead, or another role? | Do not assume the label is homogeneous. |
| CM-002 | Which first handoff/exception is worth proving? | Evidence-to-decision packet only; no public workflow claim. |
| CM-003 | What is the first delivery model: research artifact, workflow clinic, decision-packet service, or controlled proof window? | No commercial offer is assumed. |
| CM-004 | What data may be used, by whom, and under what authorization/retention rule? | Synthetic/authorized and minimum-necessary only. |
| CM-005 | Which service-level metric can be measured without creating paperwork theater or distortion? | Measure only after a baseline and participant review. |
| CM-006 | What would qualify as category leadership within a narrow cohort? | No majority-share metric is assumed. |

## 10. First research stop condition

The research must stop and return to the founder if it finds that the apparent problem is primarily one of insurance coverage, legal dispute, contractor licensing, price/lead generation, private claims data access, or a need already adequately solved by the customer’s existing contractual/project-management process. In that event, Human Blockchain should not invent a coordination layer merely to preserve the theory.
