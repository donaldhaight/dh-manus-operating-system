# Construction Management Market-Leadership and Controlled Proof Plan

**Status:** Proposed market-validation plan. It does not authorize a public launch, live insurance/claims activity, data ingestion, paid referral program, construction service, or autonomous agent workflow.

## Strategic thesis

Human Blockchain should not aim to capture “the Construction Management market.” It should test whether it can become the preferred way for a narrow, permissioned cohort to create a **source-attributed, human-reviewed internal handoff record** for one restoration coordination job.

The thesis is credible only if the record removes a decision-useful ambiguity that is not already resolved by the cohort’s existing tools and practices. Existing platforms already describe broad capabilities across documents, lifecycle/project management, cost/process coordination, restoration job management, field documentation, and workflow automation.[1] [2] [3] [4] The proposed artifact must therefore complement—not displace by assertion—those systems.

## First-offer hypothesis

| Dimension | Proposed first offer | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | *Restoration Handoff Evidence Packet — controlled proof* | Working name only; not a standard, certification, or claims service. |
| **Customer/problem** | One sponsor organization’s selected restoration project/operations handoff. | No presumed industry-wide pain point. |
| **Inputs** | Minimum-necessary, authorized/synthetic source references and selected metadata. | No unapproved claim/policyholder data, scraping, or restricted-system extraction. |
| **Output** | Human-reviewed packet showing source, contributor/role, evidence status, missing/conflicting items, decision owner, next step, and audit trail. | Not an estimate, scope, coverage view, adjustment, settlement, legal opinion, or safety/compliance certification. |
| **Delivery** | Facilitated workflow clinic plus one documented packet. | No self-service public platform claim at first. |
| **Value hypothesis** | The sponsor can identify and route a complete or incomplete handoff more clearly than in its baseline process. | Do not claim cycle-time, payment, claim, margin, or outcome improvement before evidence. |

## Gated sequence

| Gate | Required artifact | Advance condition | Stop / revise condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G0 — Scope** | One-page user/problem/geography/delivery-model charter. | CM-001, CM-006, and CM-017 are decided. | The use case requires broad market claims or undefined authority. |
| **G1 — Permission** | Source allowlist, data map, role matrix, and human-review protocol. | Sponsor authorizes the limited proof and confirms no prohibited information/action. | Data rights, contract terms, jurisdiction, or role authority are uncertain. |
| **G2 — Baseline** | Current-state handoff map and one defined clarity/rework measure. | User confirms the current process and a measurable pain/ambiguity. | The artifact would only duplicate a configured incumbent workflow. |
| **G3 — Packet** | One completed, human-reviewed internal packet and exception log. | Decision owner uses it to approve, defer, or request completion of an internal handoff. | Any output implies an external claim/technical/legal decision or lacks provenance. |
| **G4 — Repeat** | A second independent use, correction, contribution, or follow-on request. | Repeat behavior is voluntary and tied to decision utility. | Interest depends on undisclosed compensation, ranking, referral access, or unverified promises. |
| **G5 — Economic integrity** | Payer/beneficiary/conflict disclosure and qualified review where needed. | A permissible economics hypothesis preserves evidence independence. | Revenue relies on pay-to-rank, claim-linked referral compensation, investment solicitations, or implied outcome guarantees. |
| **G6 — Narrow leadership evidence** | Auditable cohort metric, inclusion/exclusion criteria, and time-bound result. | Data supports a modest preference/adoption claim inside the defined cohort. | The team substitutes traffic, content engagement, or anecdote for repeat decision utility. |

## Measures that are safe enough to test first

The proof must measure the **artifact and its internal use**, not externally determined claim or construction outcomes. These indicators are proposed metrics, not current benchmarks.

| Metric | Proposed definition | Safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| **Source traceability** | Share of packet items linked to an authorized source reference and named contributor/role. | Do not equate traceability with truth or technical sufficiency. |
| **Decision ownership** | Whether the packet identifies a named human who may approve, defer, reject, or request completion. | Do not infer legal authority from a job title. |
| **Completeness clarity** | Reviewer’s structured assessment of whether required inputs are present, missing, conflicting, or not applicable. | The team defines the checklist; it is not a universal standard. |
| **Rework signal** | Number/type of internal follow-up requests attributable to missing/unclear packet context versus baseline. | Small cohorts are directional only; do not claim statistical proof. |
| **Repeat-use signal** | Independent request to use, correct, contribute to, or sponsor a second authorized packet. | Do not treat paid placement or incentives as product-market validation. |
| **Governance adherence** | Evidence that no prohibited data/action, hidden conflict, or bypassed human gate occurred. | One breach pauses the proof pending review. |

## Category-leadership ladder

| Level | What could be said internally | What cannot yet be said |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — Research** | “We are studying a possible restoration coordination job.” | “We lead the category.” |
| **1 — Controlled proof** | “A defined cohort is testing a human-reviewed handoff packet.” | “We improve claims, outcomes, margin, safety, or compliance.” |
| **2 — Repeat evidence** | “Within the defined cohort, voluntary repeat use/correction/contribution has been observed.” | “We have market share or an industry standard.” |
| **3 — Narrow preference** | “A defined cohort prefers the artifact for a documented job, subject to stated limitations.” | “We are a marketplace, neutral authority, affiliation, or regulator.” |
| **4 — Expansion decision** | “The founder has evidence to consider a second workflow, geography, or stakeholder interface.” | “The ontology or governance has been proven at industry scale.” |

## What not to copy

Do not copy generic end-to-end project platforms, restoration ERP interfaces, vendor messaging, claims-management workflows, or credential/association language. The operating lesson from the alternatives is more useful: incumbents reveal where robust systems of record already exist. Human Blockchain’s independent contribution, if it exists, must be a governed, accountable connection between source evidence and a named human decision—not a prettier duplicate interface.

## First founder decision

**CM-006 is the next decision:** select one internal restoration handoff to observe. The default recommendation is **field documentation → internal project/operations completeness review**, because it avoids direct external claim action and can be measured without claiming to decide coverage, scope, value, safety, or compliance. This remains a proposal until a sponsor, user, state/context, and data boundary are approved.
