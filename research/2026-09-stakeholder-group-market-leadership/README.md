# Stakeholder-Group Market-Leadership Research

**Reference date:** September 5, 2026
**Status:** Two first-pass stakeholder packages are complete. They are evidence-led research and controlled-proof preparation, not market-share claims, operating companies, public directories, affiliations, offerings, or regulated services.

## Purpose

The Human Blockchain / InsuranceRestorationMarket thesis requires each stakeholder group to be assessed as a possible startup market: its customer jobs, alternatives, incentives, authority boundaries, economics hypotheses, governance risks, and evidence needed to earn durable preference. The work does **not** assume that a stakeholder label is a public category, a formed institution, or a market that Human Blockchain can control.

> **Operating rule:** Build authority through a small, source-attributed, human-accountable proof loop. Do not make a marketplace, token, affiliation, standard, API integration, referral program, or market-leadership claim before the workflow, decision rights, data permissions, and repeat-use evidence are established.

## Evidence and use boundaries

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Observed** | Directly supported by a cited primary/official source. |
| **Limited** | Source is relevant but cannot support the broader claim sometimes associated with it. |
| **Reported** | Credible but not directly revalidated; a lead, not a decision fact. |
| **Inferred / proposed** | Human Blockchain interpretation or potential offer; must be validated. |
| **Founder decision required** | A scope, economic, governance, or risk choice that research cannot make. |
| **Counsel / qualified review required** | A point that should not move into live/public use without appropriate review. |
