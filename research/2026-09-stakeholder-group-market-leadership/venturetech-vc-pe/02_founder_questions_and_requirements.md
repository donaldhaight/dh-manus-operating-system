# VC/PE Capital Layer — Founder Questions and Requirements Register

**Working entity:** United Stakeholders of America (working name)
**Purpose:** Convert the broad intent—“become the authority, seed the standard, and steer toward better processes, APIs, MCPs, and data”—into answerable decisions before public claims, capital-related features, or automation are activated.
**Status:** Discovery register. A question is not a mandate; an unanswered question remains an explicit constraint.

## How to use this register

Each question should be answered with one of five statuses: **Decided**, **Founder decision required**, **Research required**, **Legal/compliance review required**, or **Defer**. A response becomes a rule only when it has a named owner, a source or rationale, an effective date, and a review cadence. The priority scale is **P0** (must be answered before public launch), **P1** (before a paid or partner program), and **P2** (before scale).

## First five answers required before any public capital-layer launch

1. **CAP-001:** Confirm the public problem and explicit non-problems.
2. **CAP-011:** Name the first qualified audience and geography.
3. **CAP-021:** Approve the first workflow proof target.
4. **CAP-031:** Approve the minimum public taxonomy boundary.
5. **CAP-061:** Name the legal/compliance review path or retain a strictly non-investment, non-solicitation educational posture.
