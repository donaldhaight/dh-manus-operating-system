# Historical Institutional Corpus for Human Blockchain

## Purpose

This directory is a living, source-grounded study of how technology cycles have attempted to coordinate fragmented markets, identities, evidence, capital, and institutional authority. Its purpose is to improve Human Blockchain’s narrative and design discipline—not to retroactively prove the project, import a historical business model, or claim any role that has not been earned.

The core thesis tested here is that technology waves repeatedly produce the same coordination questions: **What is shared? Who may act? Who pays? Who benefits? Who decides? What evidence is required? How is error corrected?** The historical answer varies by era, but durable systems make these questions more explicit than their failed or speculative counterparts.

## Evidence rules

| Status | Meaning | Use in decisions and public materials |
|---|---|---|
| **HBOS documented** | A present working meaning or founder draft in the authoritative Human Blockchain Operating System. | Cite its repository path; distinguish it from external fact and binding decision. |
| **Directly observed** | Confirmed in a primary source, official record, standards body, regulator, or legal text. | May support a bounded factual claim with a citation. |
| **Reported** | Credible contextual material not yet independently revalidated here. | Do not use as the sole basis for a material claim. |
| **Inferred / proposed** | An analogy, design lesson, or strategy hypothesis. | Requires founder decision and, where relevant, customer, counsel, privacy, security, or compliance validation. |
| **Open decision** | An intentionally unresolved question. | Preserve it in a decision register; do not silently resolve it through generated content. |

> **History is evidence, not destiny.** A historical pattern can identify a question Human Blockchain must answer. It cannot prove demand, legality, safety, fairness, or institutional authority in the present market.

## What the corpus presently supports

The corpus supports a limited strategic proposition: Human Blockchain may earn credibility by proving a narrow, human-accountable **Evidence-to-Decision Operating Layer** for one real service-market workflow. That layer would make actor, legal entity, stakeholder group, authority, commitment, evidence, decision, exception, and learning visible. This is consistent with the Human Blockchain working vocabulary and continuity brief, but remains a proposed operating approach rather than a declared standard or institution.[^hbos-definitions] [^hbos-brief]

It does **not** support a generic marketplace, public ranking system, token or ICO, autonomous capital allocation, ungoverned agent network, public-benefit legal form, industry consortium, association, federation, foundation, regulated financial/insurance service, or claim of public authority. Each would require a separate demonstrated need, constitution/charter, rights model, economics, independent review, legal analysis, and founder decision.

## Corpus maintenance protocol

| Step | Required action |
|---|---|
| **Add evidence** | Record source URL/path, access date, claim, evidence status, restriction, and the decision it may inform. Prefer primary sources. |
| **Add interpretation** | Label it as inference/proposal, explain the boundary of the analogy, and link it to a specific decision or experiment. |
| **Correct a claim** | Preserve the prior claim in a dated change note; do not silently overwrite historical lineage. |
| **Use in an agent context pack** | Include only relevant, approved paths; preserve source links and status labels; do not turn a working draft into authority. |
| **Use in public content** | Recheck sources, reduce claims to the source-supported scope, disclose commercial relationships, and obtain required legal/compliance review. |

## Next evidence-ready action

The next strategic action is not broad platform construction. It is the founder decision identified as **HIC-001** in the pattern map: select one restoration handoff or exception that can be run as an authorized/synthetic or real controlled proof loop. Define the current baseline, actors, source evidence, service promise, decision gates, exception path, and observable outcome before attempting wider affiliation or institutional form.
