# Current Market Research and Better-Solution Case

## Purpose

This package assesses the current market around knowledge/RAG, multi-agent orchestration, work/control planes, governed automation, and restoration operations. It develops a cautious, evidence-led case for a Human Blockchain/DH Manus **Evidence-to-Decision Operating Layer**.

## Reading order

| Order | File | Role |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | `04_executive_market_case.md` | Concise strategic recommendation, differentiation thesis, first wedge, validation agenda, and decision questions. |
| 2 | `03_opportunity_assessment.md` | Detailed market comparison, risk analysis, target-user hypotheses, and proof design. |
| 3 | `notes/validated_source_notes.md` | Directly reviewed current primary-source findings and exclusions. |
| 4 | `human_blockchain_market_scan.json` | Raw six-category parallel research output. Treat its reported/assumed claims as leads that require source validation before decision use. |

## Research boundary

This is strategic market research, not an investment recommendation, legal analysis, customer validation report, compliance assessment, or sales claim. The Human Blockchain repository is the source of project-specific context; external sources are used only for bounded market-pattern and product-positioning observations.
