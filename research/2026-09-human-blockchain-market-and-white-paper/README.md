# Human Blockchain Market and White-Paper Package

## Purpose

This self-contained research package preserves two previously delivered, source-cited bodies of work in the **DH Manus Operating System** repository:

1. A CTO-perspective white paper explaining the relationship among the Human Blockchain knowledge source, the DH Manus operating-design layer, and the DH Mission Control control plane.
2. Current market research and a cautious case for a differentiated Evidence-to-Decision Operating Layer.

## Repository boundary

| Repository | Role |
|---|---|
| `human-blockchain-operating-system` | Authoritative Human Blockchain knowledge library. Project-specific context should be sourced from this repository rather than silently copied or altered here. |
| `dh-manus-operating-system` | Operating-design layer. This package belongs here because it informs strategic positioning, architecture, commissioning, and staged validation. |
| `dh-mission-control` | Working application/control plane. Application source, schema, migrations, tests, and operational documentation belong there. |

## Provenance and evidence discipline

The documents were written in the prior session and recovered unchanged from the sandbox workspace before this Git commit. Their project-specific context traces to the Human Blockchain knowledge repository; their external market observations cite the relevant public sources within the documents. The evidence labels **OBSERVED**, **REPORTED**, **INFERRED**, and **ASSUMED** are intentional and must remain intact when the material is reused.

## Operating boundary

This package is strategic research and reflection material. It is not an implementation authorization, sales promise, legal assessment, investment recommendation, or evidence that product-market fit has been achieved. The proposed Evidence-to-Decision Packet remains a hypothesis requiring a bounded, human-gated validation loop.
